You want to grow your business online but can't decide between a showcase website, an online store and an app? The right choice depends less on technology than on **what you want your customers to do**.

## The showcase website: get found and convince

A showcase site presents your company, services, work and contact details. Its goal: **generate enquiries** (calls, WhatsApp messages, forms) and reassure prospects discovering you.

**It's the right choice if:**

- you sell services (consultancy, agency, trades, clinic, school…);
- customers contact you before buying;
- you want to show up on Google in your city.

**Key points:** professional design, flawless mobile speed, pages optimised for your keywords and clear calls to action.

## The online store: sell 24/7

An online store lets customers browse your catalogue, order and pay — online or cash on delivery, still very common in Morocco.

**It's the right choice if:**

- you sell physical or digital products;
- you want to reach customers beyond your city;
- you're ready to manage orders, stock and delivery.

**Key points:** polished product pages, a simple checkout, payment methods that suit your customers and SEO for every product and category. Learn more about [e-commerce development](/en/services/ecommerce-development).

## The custom app or platform: a tool at the heart of your business

When your customers (or your team) need to **do something** repeatedly — book, track an order, manage documents, connect users — a web or mobile app starts to make sense.

**It's the right choice if:**

- your service relies on regular use (bookings, tracking, marketplace…);
- you want to automate internal tasks (HR, stock, invoicing);
- notifications, geolocation or offline access matter.

**Examples:** a services marketplace like [Bricol.clic](/en/work/bricol-clic), a document management platform like [SmartWathiqa](/en/work/smartwathiqa), or an [HR system](/en/work/sirh).

## Quick decision guide

- **You want visibility and enquiries** → showcase website.
- **You want to sell directly online** → online store.
- **You want to offer a service people use often, or digitise your processes** → custom app or platform.

## What if you start small?

Many successful projects start with a **fast, well-ranked showcase site** and then grow: a blog, a store, a client area… as long as the site is built on a solid technical base that can grow with you.

The same goes for apps: a first version (MVP) with the essential features lets you test the idea with real users before investing more.
