When someone searches "plumber Casablanca", "restaurant Agadir" or "web agency Rabat", Google first shows a map with three businesses, then the regular results. Being in those top spots means getting calls **at the exact moment a customer needs you**.

Here are 10 practical steps to improve your local SEO in Morocco.

## 1. Create and complete your Google Business Profile

This is the foundation of local SEO. Fill in **everything**: exact name, primary and secondary categories, address or service area, opening hours, phone, website, description, services and photos. A complete profile builds trust and shows up more often.

## 2. Pick the right categories

The primary category carries a lot of weight. Be specific: "Car rental agency" rather than "Company", "Dental clinic" rather than "Health".

## 3. Be consistent everywhere

Your name, address and phone number should be **identical** on your website, Google profile, social media and directories. Inconsistencies confuse Google.

## 4. Collect reviews… and reply to them

Reviews influence both rankings and customers' decisions. Ask for a review after every successful job (a simple WhatsApp link does the trick) and **reply to all of them**, including criticism, calmly and professionally.

## 5. Create a page for each city or service area

If you work in several cities, a dedicated page for each — with genuinely useful, specific content, not copy-paste — helps Google understand where you operate. For example, our page on [web development in Casablanca](/en/web-agency/casablanca).

## 6. Optimise titles and descriptions

Every page needs a unique title that includes your service and city ("4×4 rental in Dakhla — WhatsApp booking") and a description that makes people want to click.

## 7. Make your site lightning-fast on mobile

Most local searches happen on smartphones, often on mobile data. A slow site drives visitors away and hurts your rankings. Aim for pages that display in under 2–3 seconds.

## 8. Add structured data

Schema.org markup (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ…) helps Google understand your business and can enrich how your results appear.

## 9. Think multilingual

In Morocco, customers search in French, Arabic, and sometimes English or Spanish. A well-structured multilingual site with hreflang tags lets you be found in every language.

## 10. Publish useful content regularly

Blog posts, answers to common questions, local projects… Regular, useful content shows Google your site is alive and ranks you for new searches.

## Where to start?

If you only do one thing this week: **complete your Google Business Profile and ask your 5 best customers for a review**. Then work on your site's speed and local pages.
