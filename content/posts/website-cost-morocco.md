"How much does a website cost?" is the first question most businesses ask. The honest answer is **it depends** — and not as a way to dodge the question. Two sites that look alike can require very different amounts of work.

Instead of throwing out a random number, here are the 7 factors that actually move a quote, so you can compare proposals with confidence.

## 1. The type of website

This is the main factor:

- **Showcase website**: a few pages presenting your business, services and contact details. Ideal for getting found on Google and reassuring prospects.
- **Online store**: product catalogue, cart, online or cash-on-delivery payment, order and stock management.
- **Custom platform or app**: client portal, bookings, dashboards, business software… every feature is built for your needs.

Not sure which one you need? Read our guide [showcase site, online store or app: which one?](/en/blog/website-ecommerce-or-app).

## 2. Design: template or custom

A purchased theme costs less up front, but your site will look like thousands of others and is often slower. A **custom design** starts from your brand, your customers and your goals: it stands out, loads faster and converts better.

Advanced animation, motion design or 3D experiences add value… and design time.

## 3. Number of pages and languages

Every page has to be designed, written, built and optimised. In Morocco, a site in **French, Arabic and English** is often a real commercial advantage — but each language adds translation, layout work (Arabic reads right to left) and dedicated SEO.

## 4. Features

Contact form, appointment booking, online payment, member area, CRM or ERP integration, blog, multiple currencies… Each feature has a development **and** maintenance cost. List what's essential at launch and what can wait for version two.

## 5. Content

Copy, photos, videos: who creates them? Professional content, written for your customers **and** for Google, makes a huge difference. If you're starting from scratch, budget for it.

## 6. Search engine optimisation (SEO)

A website nobody finds on Google earns nothing. A good quote should spell out what's included:

- technical SEO (speed, structure, tags, sitemap, structured data);
- keyword research and on-page optimisation;
- Google Business Profile and local pages.

Learn more about [our SEO service](/en/services/seo-growth).

## 7. What happens after launch

Domain name, hosting, HTTPS certificate, backups, security updates, new features… Always ask **what's included after launch** and what will be billed separately.

## How to compare two quotes

A very low quote often hides gaps: no SEO, a generic theme, no training, slow hosting, or a site you don't really own. Ask these questions:

1. Is the design custom or based on a template?
2. Will the site be fast on mobile? How is that measured?
3. Which SEO tasks are included?
4. Can I edit my content myself?
5. Do I own the site, the code and the domain name?
6. What support is included after launch?
